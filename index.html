ACTION & TIMING

Your streets

Hold the screen. Drag to aim. Let go to shoot.

Play again from street I. Your stars stay saved.

HOW TO PLAY

One thumb

1. Hold the screen. Time slows. A gold dotted line shows where the bullet will go — including bounces.

2. Drag that line onto a skillet, barrel, or tin sign so it kicks into the man behind cover. Let go to fire.

3. Metal bounces the shot. Wood stops it. If a man ducks, bounce around the cover — wait until the line says WILL HIT or BOUNCE WILL HIT, then let go.

4. Kill everyone before they shoot you. Tap the six-shot cylinder to reload.

Stars: clear the street · extra star if you use few shots · three stars for one bullet.
